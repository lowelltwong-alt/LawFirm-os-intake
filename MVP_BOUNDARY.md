# MVP Boundary

## In scope

- synthetic source bundles;
- local CLI and filesystem artifacts;
- practice-profile resolution;
- structural source segmentation;
- candidate party, matter, posture, and deadline extraction;
- independent evidence critique and escalation recommendation;
- human confirmation artifact;
- conflict-search seed without conclusion;
- proposed legal budget with deterministic calculations;
- matter-opening readiness blocked pending human/legal gates;
- schemas, worker manifests, tests, evidence graph, and run ledger.

## Out of scope

- real/public case ingestion at runtime;
- production model calls;
- external connectors or writes;
- conflict clearance;
- engagement or declination;
- deadline docketing;
- matter/iManage opening;
- budget approval/submission;
- automatic profile learning;
- dynamic agents;
- graph database or generalized RAG.

The MVP is successful when it makes a reviewer faster without changing legal authority.
