# Decision Trace

## Situation

What constraint, defect, risk, or opportunity triggered this change?

## Decision

What is changing?

## Non-decision

What is intentionally not changing?

## Authority impact

Which repo owns the controlling contract? Is this local candidate work or a promoted contract change?

## Evidence

Which fixtures, source refs, tests, reports, and prior decisions support the change?

## Alternatives rejected

What other options were considered and why were they rejected?

## Risks and rollback

What could fail? How is the change contained or reversed?

## Validation

List exact commands and results.

## Human gates

What review or approval remains required?
