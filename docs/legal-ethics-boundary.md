# Legal and Professional-Responsibility Boundary

This repository is technical workflow scaffolding, not legal advice.

## Human accountability

Lawyers and authorized firm personnel remain responsible for:

- prospective-client communications;
- conflict analysis;
- identifying the client and scope of representation;
- confidentiality and privilege decisions;
- legal deadlines;
- engagement or declination;
- legal strategy and work product;
- staffing and budget approval;
- submissions to clients, carriers, courts, or third parties.

## Confidentiality

Do not use real client or prospective-client data until the firm has approved the model/provider, retention, access, security, vendor, consent, and supervision posture. Public availability does not eliminate professional or privacy considerations.

## Supervision

Model and specialist outputs must be reviewable. Review packets should expose source coverage, evidence, context influence, alternatives, missing information, and prohibited actions.

## Third-party payer and instructing source

The workflow must not infer the represented client from who sent the matter, pays the bill, or provides guidelines. Those relationships are separately confirmed.

## No reliance state

Every generated legal classification, date, conflict seed, and budget remains a proposal until reviewed under firm policy.

Production deployment requires counsel to review jurisdiction-specific professional-responsibility obligations and firm policy.
